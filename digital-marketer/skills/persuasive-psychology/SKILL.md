# Marketing Master Class: Persuasive Psychology

Humans do not make rational purchasing decisions. They make emotional decisions and justify them with logic. You must master cognitive biases to trigger action.

## ✅ ALWAYS Use Cognitive Triggers

1.  **Price Anchoring**:
    -   Never show a $99 price tag alone. Show a $299 "Enterprise" plan next to it, and a $19 "Basic" plan. The $99 plan suddenly looks like the most reasonable and safe choice (The Goldilocks Effect).

2.  **Scarcity and Urgency**:
    -   *Real Scarcity*: "Only 4 spots left in this cohort." 
    -   *Urgency*: "Offer expires at midnight." 
    -   If the user believes they can buy it tomorrow for the same price, they will procrastinate forever.

3.  **Social Proof (The Bandwagon Effect)**:
    -   We look to others to know what to do.
    -   "Join 10,432 other founders" is infinitely more powerful than "Join our newsletter."

4.  **Risk Reversal**:
    -   Buying causes psychological friction (Fear of Loss). 
    -   Eliminate this with an absurd guarantee: "Try it for 30 days. If you don't double your leads, I'll refund you and pay you $100 for your time."

## ❌ NEVER

-   **Fake Scarcity**: Never use a countdown timer that resets every time the page is refreshed. Users are smart, and fake scarcity destroys brand trust permanently.
-   **Information Overload (Paradox of Choice)**: Never give a user 10 different pricing options. They will experience decision fatigue and buy nothing. Give them 3 options tops.
