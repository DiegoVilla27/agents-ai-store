# Marketing Master Class: Performance Marketing (Paid Ads)

Performance marketing is buying data. You put money into a machine to figure out exactly who buys and for how much.

## ✅ ALWAYS

1.  **Structure Campaigns by Funnel Stage**:
    -   **Top of Funnel (TOFU)**: Cold traffic. Broad targeting. Goal: Awareness and cheap clicks/video views. 
    -   **Middle of Funnel (MOFU)**: Warm traffic. Lookalike audiences or people who engaged with TOFU. Goal: Lead generation / Email capture.
    -   **Bottom of Funnel (BOFU)**: Hot traffic. Retargeting (abandoned cart, visited pricing page). Goal: Direct Sales (High ROAS).

2.  **Match Intent with Platform**:
    -   *Google Ads (Search)*: High Intent. The user is actively searching for "plumber near me". Bid aggressively. Use highly relevant text copy.
    -   *Meta/TikTok Ads (Social)*: Low Intent. The user is watching cat videos. You must disrupt their scroll with a highly visual, emotionally engaging Hook.

3.  **Optimize the Creative (Ad)**:
    -   The algorithm does the targeting now. The *Creative is the Targeting*. 
    -   Always test 3-5 different creatives (Images vs. UGC Videos vs. Motion Graphics) against the same audience to see what resonates.

## ❌ NEVER

-   **Focus on Vanity Metrics**: Do not optimize for CTR (Click-Through Rate) or CPC (Cost Per Click) if the ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) is negative. A high CTR with 0 sales means your ad is clickbait and your landing page is failing. Focus on CPA (Cost Per Acquisition).
-   **Change Budgets Drastically**: Never scale a winning ad campaign by increasing the budget by 200% in one day. You will break the algorithm's learning phase. Scale by 15-20% every few days.
