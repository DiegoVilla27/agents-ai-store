# Marketing Master Class: Email Marketing & Automation

Social media followers are rented audiences; algorithms can crush your reach overnight. An email list is an owned asset. It is the most profitable channel in digital marketing.

## ✅ ALWAYS

1.  **The Soap Opera Sequence (Welcome Flow)**:
    -   When someone subscribes, do not just send "Here is your 10% off code."
    -   Send a 5-day automated sequence that tells the founder's story, builds high drama, and ends with a strong pitch on Day 5. Build a relationship.

2.  **Segment by Behavior (RFM Analysis)**:
    -   *Recency, Frequency, Monetary value*.
    -   Do not send the same blast to everyone. If a user hasn't opened an email in 90 days, put them in a "Win-Back" sequence. If they buy every week, put them in a VIP segment with exclusive offers.

3.  **Optimize the Subject Line**:
    -   If they don't open the email, the copy inside doesn't matter.
    -   Use curiosity ("The weird reason your ads are failing..."), personalization ("Diego, quick question"), or urgency ("Ending in 3 hours").

## ❌ NEVER

-   **Buy Email Lists**: Never scrape or buy lists. It destroys domain reputation, and your emails will be permanently marked as spam by Gmail/Outlook.
-   **Use No-Reply Addresses**: Never send emails from `noreply@company.com`. It tells the user "We want to talk at you, but we don't want to hear from you." Use a real name: `diego@company.com`.
