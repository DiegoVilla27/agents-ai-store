# Marketing Master Class: Direct Response Copywriting

Direct Response is not branding. It is salesmanship in print. If the reader does not take action, the copy failed.

## ✅ ALWAYS Use Frameworks

1.  **PAS (Problem - Agitation - Solution)**:
    -   *Problem*: Identify the exact pain point (e.g., "Tired of spending 10 hours a week on accounting?").
    -   *Agitation*: Rub salt in the wound (e.g., "Every hour spent in Excel is an hour you aren't growing your business or spending with your family.").
    -   *Solution*: Present your product as the only logical cure (e.g., "AutoBooks automates your ledger in 5 minutes a week.").

2.  **AIDA (Attention - Interest - Desire - Action)**:
    -   *Attention*: A scroll-stopping hook.
    -   *Interest*: A counter-intuitive fact or compelling story.
    -   *Desire*: Paint a picture of the dream outcome (Benefits).
    -   *Action*: A clear, singular command ("Click here to get 50% off").

## ❌ NEVER

-   **Focus on Features**: Nobody cares that your software is "built with React" or has a "multi-threaded backend". They care that it "loads instantly so you never lose a frustrated customer."
-   **Use Passive Voice**: "Our product can be used to save time" is weak. Use active voice: "Save 10 hours a week with our product."
-   **Clever over Clear**: Do not use confusing puns or abstract metaphors in Headlines. If a 5th grader cannot understand what you sell in 3 seconds, rewrite it.
