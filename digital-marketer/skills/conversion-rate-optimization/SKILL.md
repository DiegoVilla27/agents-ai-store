# Marketing Master Class: Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

CRO is the science of turning traffic into money. Traffic is useless if the bucket is leaking.

## ✅ ALWAYS

1.  **Above the Fold Optimization**:
    -   The most critical real estate. It MUST contain: 
        1. A clear Headline (What is it?).
        2. A Subheadline (Why should I care?).
        3. A Hero Image or Video (Showing the product in action).
        4. A high-contrast Call to Action (CTA).

2.  **Reduce Friction & Cognitive Load**:
    -   Every extra form field drops conversion by 10%. If you only need an email, ONLY ask for an email.
    -   Remove external links from landing pages. A landing page has ONE goal (buy/subscribe). Do not distract the user with navigation menus or blog links.

3.  **CTA Architecture**:
    -   CTAs must look clickable (buttons, not text links).
    -   Use action-oriented verbs. Instead of "Submit", use "Get My Free Report" or "Start My 14-Day Trial".
    -   Place CTAs near the bottom of long scrolling sections (don't make the user scroll back up to buy).

## ❌ NEVER

-   **Guess**: CRO is based on data (Heatmaps, Scrollmaps, Google Analytics). Always propose A/B testing changes.
-   **Ignore Mobile**: 70%+ of traffic is mobile. If the CTA is not visible on a mobile screen without scrolling, the CRO is failing.
